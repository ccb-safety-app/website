A certificate for everyone on your list, in one click.

Certificate Maker is a Google Sheets add-on that turns your list of names into beautiful, personalized PDF certificates. It saves them to Drive and emails each person theirs, and you never leave your sheet.

See pricing

Five steps

Open it from Extensions in any Google Sheet, including a sheet of Google Form responses.

  1. 1

    Pick a design

    51 designs in 12 kinds, five colors, US Letter or A4. Or bring your own Google Slides design with placeholders like {{Name}}.

  2. 2

    Match your columns

    Name, course and date are found for you. Type anything that's the same for everyone (organization, signer) just once.

  3. 3

    Check the names

    Name Check finds long, empty, repeated and oddly capitalized names and fixes them in your sheet when you approve.

  4. 4

    Add logos and signatures

    Optional. Upload a logo, and up to three signatures drawn or uploaded.

  5. 5

    Make and send

    Choose email, a QR code and file names, then watch every certificate go out. Each one is saved to Drive, and the link, number and status are written back to your sheet.

51 designs that look properly made

Awards, courses, quizzes, graduation, sports, gift vouchers and more. Each one opens as a normal Google Slides file, so you can change fonts, add your logo or move things around.

Small things that save you an evening

  • A preview with real namesFlip through your people and see each certificate before anything is sent, then get an exact preview from Slides.
  • Long names fitNames like Bartholomew Montgomery-Fitzwilliam shrink to fit the line instead of spilling off the page.
  • A QR code anyone can checkScanning it opens the original PDF in your Drive, so employers and parents can see it's real. Every certificate also gets a number, like NCC-2026-0042.
  • Quizzes: only people who passLink a Google Forms quiz, set a pass mark, and each passing response gets its certificate within a minute.
  • No stuck runsGmail limits how many emails you can send a day. When a run needs more, the rest go out later by themselves.
  • Print them all at onceOne PDF with every certificate, plus optional PNG copies people can post on LinkedIn.
