How to use Certificate Maker

Make a personalized certificate for everyone on a Google Sheet, then save them to Drive or email each person theirs. The panel walks you through five steps.

  • Works inGoogle Sheets
  • Set-up timeAbout 5 minutes

Step 1 of 7

Put your names in a sheet and open the add-on

Use one row per person, with columns such as Name, Email and Course. A Google Form's response sheet works too.

  1. Click Extensions, then Certificate Maker, then Open Certificate Maker.
  2. The preview at the top shows a real certificate for the first row. Use the arrows to see other people.
  3. No sheet yet? Choose Add a Sample Sheet.
If it doesn't work
  • The panel says "Signed in to more than one Google account?" Google add-ons can mix up accounts when several are signed in to the same browser. Open the file in a private window signed in to only one account, or sign out of the others, then open the add-on again.

Step 2 of 7

Pick a design

Pick a category, then a design. Choose a color and the paper size (US Letter or A4).

  1. Choose a Category, such as Completion or Achievement & Award.
  2. Click a design in the gallery.
  3. Pick a Color and Paper size.

Step 3 of 7

Use your own Google Slides design (optional)

Already have a certificate in Google Slides? Type placeholders like {{Name}} and {{Course}} where the details go, then choose it here.

  1. Click My Slides Design.
  2. Click Choose from Google Drive and pick your presentation.
  3. The panel lists the placeholders it found.
If it doesn't work
  • "That presentation could not be opened." Choose it with Choose from Google Drive rather than pasting the link. That gives Certificate Maker permission to open that one file.
  • No placeholders found. Type them with double curly brackets, exactly like {{Name}}, in a text box on the first slide.

Step 4 of 7

Fill in the details

Tell Certificate Maker which column holds each detail, or type text that's the same for everyone.

  1. Pick the Sheet with your names.
  2. For each detail, choose a column or type the text.
  3. Set the Date: today, a fixed date, or a column.

Step 5 of 7

Check the names

Certificate Maker looks for names typed in all capitals or all lower case, and names too long for the line.

  1. Choose Fix Them to correct capital letters, such as maria lopez to Maria Lopez.
  2. Long names shrink to fit. Check the ones listed.

Step 6 of 7

Add your logo and signatures

Add up to three signers. Each has a name, a title and a signature you draw or upload. You can also add a logo and a seal.

  1. Type the signer's Name and Title.
  2. Draw the signature, or upload a photo of it. Turn on Remove Background to drop the paper color.
  3. Click Upload under Logo to add your logo.
If it doesn't work
  • The signature has a gray box around it. Turn on Remove Background, or upload a PNG with a clear background.

Step 7 of 7

Choose delivery and make them

Choose what happens to each certificate. They're always saved as PDFs in your Drive.

  1. Turn on Email Each Person Their Certificate to send them out.
  2. Keep QR Code to Check It's Genuine on, so anyone can confirm a certificate is real.
  3. Turn on Number Each Certificate for an ID like CERT-2026-0001.
  4. Click Preview to see exactly how the first one looks, then Make Certificates.
If it doesn't work
  • "Free Left This Month: 0". The free plan makes 5 certificates a month. They come back on the 1st, or Pro makes unlimited certificates.
  • Emails stopped partway. Google limits how many emails an account can send each day. The rest are sent when you run it again tomorrow.
  • "Couldn't Save to Drive". Google had a short problem. Wait a minute and click Make Certificates again. Finished ones aren't made twice.
