How to use Form Payments

Ask for payment with every Google Form response. Each person gets an email with an itemized total and buttons to pay by card or PayPal, and you see who's paid.

  • Works inGoogle Forms
  • Set-up timeAbout 10 minutes
  • Free plan10 payment requests a month

Step 1 of 7

Open Form Payments in your form

Open your form in edit mode. Your form needs a way to know each person's email: turn on Collect email addresses in the form's Settings, or ask for it in a question.

  1. Click the puzzle piece (Add-ons) at the top of the form.
  2. Choose Form Payments, then Open Form Payments.
  3. Go to the Setup tab.
If it doesn't work
  • The panel says "Signed in to more than one Google account?" Google add-ons can mix up accounts when several are signed in to the same browser. Open the file in a private window signed in to only one account, or sign out of the others, then open the add-on again.

Step 2 of 7

Connect how you get paid

Money goes straight to your own Stripe or PayPal account. Form Payments never takes a cut.

  1. Stripe: create a restricted key in your Stripe dashboard with write access to Products, Prices and Payment Links and read access to Checkout Sessions, then paste it.
  2. PayPal: paste your PayPal app's Client ID and Secret.
  3. Other Ways to Pay (optional): add your Cash App, Venmo or Zelle details, or say "pay at the door".
If it doesn't work
  • "Couldn't Reach Stripe". Check the key has the permissions above, and that it's a live key (rk_live…) if you want real payments. Test keys (rk_test…) only take test cards.

Step 3 of 7

Set your prices

Prices come from the answers: a price for each choice, multiplied by a quantity question if you have one.

  1. Pick the question and type a price next to each choice.
  2. Optional: Everyone Pays a Fixed Amount, Quantity From a question, add-ons, or Let People Choose the Amount for donations.
If it doesn't work
  • A question isn't listed. Prices work with multiple-choice, checkbox, dropdown, scale and short-answer (number) questions.

Step 4 of 7

Tax, fees and discount codes (optional)

Add tax, pass the card fee on to the payer, or create discount codes with limits.

  1. Turn on Add Tax and type the rate.
  2. Turn on Payer Covers the Card Fee if you want.
  3. Under Discount Codes, pick the question with the code and add codes.

Step 5 of 7

Write the emails

Each person gets a payment request with an itemized total. Unpaid people can get gentle reminders, and everyone gets a receipt when they pay.

  1. Set the Sender Name, Replies Go To and Subject.
  2. Edit the message. Tags like Name and Total are filled in for each person.
  3. Click Send Me a Test to see it in your inbox.
  4. Turn on Remind People Who Haven't Paid and choose how often.
If it doesn't work
  • Emails aren't arriving. Check the person's spam folder, and that your form collects email addresses.

Step 6 of 7

Show prices on the form

Let people see prices while they fill in the form, and tell them to watch for the payment email.

  1. Turn on Show Prices Under Priced Questions.
  2. Keep Tell People to Check Their Email on.
  3. Click Save Changes.

Step 7 of 7

See who's paid

The Payments tab shows what's been collected and who's still waiting. Everything is also kept in a spreadsheet called Payments: (your form) in your Drive.

  1. Click Check Now to look for new payments. Form Payments also checks every hour.
  2. Paid in cash or check? Open the person and choose Mark as Paid. They get a receipt.
  3. Click Open Sheet for the full record.
If it doesn't work
  • Someone paid but still shows as waiting. Click Check Now. Card payments can take a minute to show.
  • I need to stop one request. Open it and choose Cancel. Its payment links stop working.
