How to use Form Limits

Give each choice in your Google Form a number of places, close the form after a number of responses or at a set time, and keep a real waitlist.

  • Set-up timeAbout 3 minutes

Step 1 of 7

Open Form Limits in your form

Open your form in edit mode. It needs at least one multiple-choice, dropdown or checkbox question for choice limits.

  1. Click the puzzle piece (Add-ons) at the top of the form.
  2. Choose Form Limits, then Open Form Limits.
If it doesn't work
  • The panel says "Signed in to more than one Google account?" Google add-ons can mix up accounts when several are signed in to the same browser. Open the file in a private window signed in to only one account, or sign out of the others, then open the add-on again.

Step 2 of 7

Set places for each choice

Pick a question, then give every choice the same number of places, or a number for each choice.

  1. Open Choice Limits.
  2. Choose Same for All or Each Choice and type the number of places.
  3. Under When a Choice Is Full, choose Remove It or Offer a Waitlist.
  4. Keep Show Places Left on so people see "(3 left)".
If it doesn't work
  • My question isn't listed. Choice limits work on multiple-choice, dropdown and checkbox questions.

Step 3 of 7

Close after a number of responses (optional)

Stop the whole form once it has enough responses, such as 40 seats in a room.

  1. Open Response Limit and turn on Close After a Number of Responses.
  2. Type the number.

Step 4 of 7

Open and close at set times (optional)

Open the form at a set time, close it at another, or keep weekly hours, like office hours that start fresh each week.

  1. Open Open and Close Times.
  2. Turn on Open at a Set Time and/or Close at a Set Time and pick the date and time.
  3. For a weekly routine, turn on Weekly Opening Hours.
If it doesn't work
  • "The closing time is in the past." Pick a later time, or switch Close at a Set Time off.
  • Times are an hour off. Times use the time zone shown under the settings. Check your computer's time zone.

Step 5 of 7

Write the closed message

Choose what people see when the form is full or not open yet.

  1. Open When the Form Is Closed.
  2. Edit the message for when it's full, and the one shown before it opens.

Step 6 of 7

Choose your emails

Get an email when the form closes, when a choice fills or when it's 80% full. Waitlisted people can be emailed when a place opens up.

  1. Open Emails and switch on the ones you want.
  2. For waitlist emails, pick the question that asks for their email address.
If it doesn't work
  • Waitlisted people aren't emailed. Turn on Collect email addresses in the form's Settings, or add a short-answer email question and choose it here.

Step 7 of 7

Switch limits on and share

Save, then share your form. Form Limits keeps it up to date as responses come in.

  1. Click Switch Limits On (or Save and Apply after changes).
  2. Open Share for the link and a QR code for posters.
  3. Activity lists what happened, such as "Sat 9:00 AM filled up".
If it doesn't work
  • A full choice still shows. The form updates a moment after each response. Reload the form to see it.
  • I want the form back to normal. Click Switch Limits Off. Your choices return to how they were.
