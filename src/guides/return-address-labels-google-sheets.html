Guides

Return address labels from Google Sheets

Return address labels are small, so a sheet holds 60 or 80 of them. Usually you want the same address on every label, which is the one thing a normal mail merge is bad at. Here's how to do it either way.

Which label sheet?

SheetLabel sizePer sheetGood for
Avery 5167 (also 8167, 5267)1-3/4" × 1/2"80Three short lines
Avery 5195 (also 8195)1-3/4" × 2/3"60Three or four lines, or a small logo
Avery 5160 (also 8160)2-5/8" × 1"30Long addresses or a larger logo

The product number is printed on the label package. 5167 labels are only half an inch tall, so keep to a name, street and city line at 7–8 pt.

The same address on every label

  1. In Spreadsheet Labels, choose the sheet (for example Avery 5167) and pick One label as the source.
  2. Type your return address once.
  3. Under Copies, enter a full sheet (80 for 5167, 60 for 5195), then click Create labels.

Without an add-on, make one label in a Google Docs table cell, then copy and paste it into every cell. It works, but it takes a while for 80 labels, and the table needs the exact measurements for that sheet.

A different return address per row

Useful when you mail for several people or businesses from one list. Keep each sender's address in its own row in Google Sheets, then choose Sheet rows as the source and set how many copies of each row you need under Copies.

Add a small logo or picture

On 5195 and 5160 labels there's room for a small logo next to or above the text. In Spreadsheet Labels, open Pictures and codes, click Choose picture and pick a PNG, JPG or GIF; a square logo with a transparent background looks best.

Printing tips for small labels

  1. Print at Actual size / 100%. On half-inch labels even a 3% shrink pushes the last rows off their labels.
  2. Test on plain paper and hold it against the label sheet before printing the real thing.
  3. Partly used sheet? Start at the first free label instead of wasting the sheet (Spreadsheet Labels' Start position setting).
