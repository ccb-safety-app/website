Form Payments privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what Form Payments for Google Forms ("Form Payments", "we") does with your information and your respondents' information. Form Payments is made by Northpine Apps.

What Form Payments can access

  • The Google Form you open it in (and only that form): its questions and responses, to work out what each person owes and where to email them. With your permission it adds price lines to questions and changes the message shown after submitting.
  • The form's response spreadsheet, or a spreadsheet it creates, where it keeps a "Payments" tab with each response's amount and payment status.
  • Your Google account email address, to send you payment summaries if you ask, and to check whether you have bought Pro.

Your Stripe and PayPal keys

The Stripe key and PayPal credentials you connect are stored in your own Google account (Apps Script user properties) and are sent only to Stripe and PayPal, to create payment links and invoices and to check whether they have been paid. We do not run a server that receives them.

What we do with it

Respondents' names, email addresses and answers are used only to create their payment request, send it from your own Gmail account, and track payment. For each payment request, the amount, the items, the payer's email address and a response reference are sent to Stripe and/or PayPal, which process payments as your payment providers under their own terms. Card details are entered only on Stripe's or PayPal's own pages and never reach Form Payments.

We do not copy, store, sell or share your form data. Your settings are stored in your own Google account and can be removed by uninstalling the add-on.

Services Form Payments uses

  • Stripe and PayPal, using your own accounts, as described above.
  • Google Forms, Sheets and Gmail, through Google Apps Script, in your own account.
  • Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider and reseller for Form Payments Pro. If you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy processes your payment and email address under its own privacy policy. To confirm your plan, Form Payments sends your license key and account email to Lemon Squeezy.

Google user data

Form Payments' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your permission, for security reasons, or where required by law.

How we protect your data

  • Your data stays in your Google account. Form Payments runs on Google Apps Script inside your own Google account. Your form, its responses and your payment list are stored and processed only by Google's own systems, which encrypt data in transit and at rest. Northpine Apps has no server that receives, copies or stores them.
  • Your Stripe and PayPal keys are protected. They are stored only in your own private Google user storage, which only your account can read, and they are sent only to Stripe and PayPal, over HTTPS, to create payment links and invoices and to check whether they were paid. We never receive them, and card numbers never reach Form Payments: payers enter them on Stripe's or PayPal's own pages. We recommend a Stripe restricted key with only the permissions Form Payments needs.
  • Encrypted connections only. Every request Form Payments makes outside Google (to Stripe, PayPal and the license check with Lemon Squeezy) uses HTTPS. Lemon Squeezy receives only your license key and account email.
  • Least access. Form Payments asks for the narrowest permissions it can. Its form permission covers only the form you open it in. It uses its spreadsheet permission only to open the form's response spreadsheet, or the one it creates, to keep your Payments tab. It sends only the payment request, reminder and receipt emails you switch on, and it has no access to your Drive files, contacts or inbox.
  • Private settings. Your prices and settings are kept in the add-on's script storage for your form. Your license key, plan and payment keys are kept in Google's per-user script storage, which only your own account can read. Respondents cannot see them, and neither can we.
  • No human access. Nobody at Northpine Apps can see your Google data. We would only ever look at data you choose to send us yourself, for example in a support email.
  • Protected developer accounts. The accounts used to build and publish Form Payments are protected with two-step verification, and the code is kept in a private repository.
  • If something goes wrong. If we learn of a security problem that affects your data, we will fix it promptly and tell affected users by email.

Keeping and deleting data

Form Payments does not keep a copy of your Google data. Each payment request adds one row to the Payments tab in your own spreadsheet: the payer's name and email, items, amount, status, the payment links and a response reference. Those rows stay in your spreadsheet until you delete them, and Stop Collecting keeps them. Your settings and discount-code counts are kept in your own Google account. Choose Disconnect next to Stripe or PayPal to delete that key from your account's storage; Remove Key does the same for your license key. A PayPal access token or license check result is cached by Google for up to six hours, then expires. Removing the add-on's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops it reading your form or sheet. Stripe and PayPal keep payment records under their own policies, and purchase records for Pro are kept by Lemon Squeezy as required for tax and accounting; to ask for those to be deleted, email us.

AI and machine learning

We do not use Google Workspace data, or any data obtained through Google APIs, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine learning models.

Your choices

Choose "Stop collecting payments" in the add-on to remove its triggers and the changes it made to your form, and disconnect Stripe or PayPal to delete your stored keys. You can stop all access by uninstalling it or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The Payments sheet stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Children

Form Payments is not directed at children under 13.
