Support

Answers to common questions about Label Maker. If yours isn't here, email {{EMAIL}}. We reply within 2 business days.

Open Label Maker

In Google Sheets, click Extensions → Label Maker → Create labels. The panel opens beside your sheet.

To try it without your own data, click Extensions → Label Maker → Add a sample sheet.

My labels don't line up

  • Check that you chose the same product number as the one on your package of labels.
  • In the print window, set the scale to Actual size (100%), not "Fit to page".
  • Print the test page on plain paper, hold it against a label sheet, and use Printer alignment in the panel to shift the layout if your printer is off.

The panel shows an error as soon as it opens

Signed in to more than one Google account? Open this sheet in a window signed in to only one account, then try again. Google can mix up accounts when several are signed in at once.

Activate Pro

After you buy Pro, your license key arrives by email. In the Label Maker panel, open Plan details and privacy, paste the key and click Activate.

To use Pro on a different Google account, click Remove key on the old account first, then activate the key on the new one.

Manage or cancel Pro yearly

Change your card, download invoices or cancel at northpineapps.lemonsqueezy.com/billing. If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the year you paid for.

Refunds

Email {{EMAIL}} within 7 days of purchase for a full refund.

Missing label size

Add your own size in the panel under Choose label sheet → Add your own size, or email us the product number and we'll look at adding it.

Uninstall

In Google Sheets, click Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, find Label Maker, and choose Uninstall. Label documents you created stay in your Google Drive.
