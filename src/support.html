Spreadsheet Labels (Google Sheets)

Open Spreadsheet Labels

In Google Sheets, click Extensions → Spreadsheet Labels → Create labels. The panel opens beside your sheet. To try it without your own data, click Extensions → Spreadsheet Labels → Add a sample sheet.

My labels don't line up

  • Check that you chose the same product number as the one on your package of labels.
  • In the print window, set the scale to Actual size (100%), not "Fit to page".
  • Print the test page on plain paper, hold it against a label sheet, and use Printer Alignment in the panel to shift the layout if your printer is off.

Missing label size

Add your own size in the panel under Choose Your Label Sheet → Add Your Own Size, or email us the product number and we'll look at adding it.

Activate Pro

After you buy Pro, your license key arrives by email. In the Spreadsheet Labels panel, open Plan Details and Privacy, paste the key and click Activate.

Form Limits (Google Forms)

Open Form Limits

Open your form in the Google Forms editor, click the Add-ons (puzzle piece) icon at the top, then Form Limits → Open Form Limits. The panel opens beside your form.

My form didn't close or open on time

  • Check the time zone shown under Open and Close Times; the times you set use that time zone.
  • Opening and closing are done by Google in the background and can take a few minutes after the set time.
  • Close the form straight away with Close Now in the panel.

The places left look wrong

Counts are rebuilt from the form's real responses each time, so deleted responses free up their places. If something still looks off, email us with the form's name and what you expected to see.

Form Payments (Google Forms)

Open Form Payments

Open your form in the Google Forms editor, click the Add-ons (puzzle piece) icon at the top, then Form Payments → Open Form Payments. Start on the Setup tab.

Connect Stripe or PayPal

Under Connect How You Get Paid, choose Connect Stripe and paste a restricted key from your Stripe dashboard, or Connect PayPal and paste your PayPal client ID and secret. Money goes straight to your own account; we never take a cut. To try it without real money, use Stripe's test mode and the test card 4242 4242 4242 4242.

Someone paid but still shows as Waiting

Paid status is checked every hour. To check right away, press Check Now on the Payments tab. For cash or check, open the person and choose Paid in Person.

Stop taking payments

On the Setup tab, press Stop Collecting Payments. The Payments spreadsheet stays in your Google Drive.

Certificate Maker (Google Sheets)

Open Certificate Maker

In Google Sheets, click Extensions → Certificate Maker → Open Certificate Maker. To try it with sample names, click Extensions → Certificate Maker → Add a Sample Sheet. Then go through the five steps: Design, Details, Name Check, Logo & Signatures, Delivery.

Some emails haven't gone out yet

Google limits how many emails an account can send each day. When the limit is reached, Certificate Maker sends the rest later by itself, as soon as Google allows. The certificates themselves are already in your Google Drive.

Send certificates automatically after a Google Forms quiz

Link the form to your sheet (in Google Forms: Responses → Link to Sheets), then in the Delivery step turn on Send Automatically When a Form Is Submitted. Turn on Only People Who Pass to send certificates only to people who reach the pass mark.

All add-ons

The panel shows an error as soon as it opens

Signed in to more than one Google account? Open the sheet or form in a window signed in to only one account, then try again. Google can mix up accounts when several are signed in at once.

"Sorry, unable to open the file at this time" after installing

This page comes from Google Drive, not from the add-on, and usually means several Google accounts are signed in to the browser. The add-on is normally installed anyway: open a sheet or form and look under Extensions. If it isn't there, open a private (Incognito) window, sign in to only the account you want to use, and install again from the Google Workspace Marketplace.

Activate Pro

After you buy Pro, your license key arrives by email. In Form Limits, Form Payments or Certificate Maker, click Free Plan at the top of the panel, paste the key under Already Bought? Paste Your License Key and click Activate. For Spreadsheet Labels, see above.

To use Pro on a different Google account, click Remove Key on the old account first, then activate the key on the new one.

Manage or cancel Pro yearly

Change your card, download invoices or cancel at northpineapps.lemonsqueezy.com/billing. If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the year you paid for.

Refunds

Uninstall

Remove an add-on's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. In Google Sheets you can also click Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, find the add-on and choose Uninstall. Files the add-on created stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.
