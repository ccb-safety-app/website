Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what Label Maker for Google Sheets ("Label Maker", "we") does with your information. Label Maker is made by Northpine Apps.

What Label Maker can access

  • The Google Sheet you open it in (and only that sheet), to read the names and addresses you want to print.
  • Google Docs that Label Maker creates for your labels. It does not open or change your other documents.
  • Your Google account email address, to show which account you are using and to check whether you have bought Pro.

What we do with it

Your addresses are used only to build your label document. Label Maker runs inside your own Google account: we do not run a server that receives your sheet, and we do not copy, store, sell or share your addresses.

Your settings (chosen label size, layout, logo, saved drafts) are stored in your own Google account and can be removed by uninstalling the add-on.

Services Label Maker uses

  • Google Maps (through Google Apps Script), only when you use "Check addresses". The addresses being checked are sent to Google to look them up.
  • Picture links: if your sheet contains links to pictures and you choose to add them to labels, Label Maker downloads those pictures from the links you provided.
  • Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider and reseller. If you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy processes your payment and email address under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. To confirm your plan, Label Maker sends only your license key and your account email to Lemon Squeezy.

Google user data

Label Maker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your permission, for security reasons, or where required by law.

How we protect your data

  • Your data stays in your Google account. Label Maker runs on Google Apps Script inside your own Google account. Your sheet, your addresses and the label documents are stored and processed only by Google's own systems, which encrypt data in transit and at rest. Northpine Apps has no server that receives, copies or stores them.
  • Encrypted connections only. Every request Label Maker makes outside Google (the license check with Lemon Squeezy, and picture links you add yourself) uses HTTPS, so it is encrypted in transit. No addresses or sheet contents are sent to Lemon Squeezy.
  • Least access. Label Maker asks for the narrowest permissions it can. Its spreadsheet permission covers only the spreadsheet you are using it in. Its Google Docs permission is used only to create and fill in your label documents; it never opens your other documents. It has no access to your email, contacts or other Drive files.
  • Private settings. Your settings and license key are kept in Google's per-user script storage, which only your own account can read. Other users of the same sheet or add-on cannot see them, and neither can we.
  • No human access. Nobody at Northpine Apps can see your Google data. We would only ever look at data you choose to send us yourself, for example in a support email.
  • Protected developer accounts. The accounts we use to build, sell and publish our add-ons are protected with strong, unique passwords and two-step verification wherever the service offers it.
  • If something goes wrong. If we learn of a security problem that affects your data, we will fix it promptly and tell affected users by email.

Keeping and deleting data

Label Maker does not keep a copy of your Google data. Your settings stay in your own Google account until you uninstall the add-on or remove its access, which deletes them. Label documents stay in your Google Drive until you delete them. Address check results are cached by Google for up to six hours to avoid repeat lookups, then expire. Purchase records are kept by Lemon Squeezy as required for tax and accounting; to ask for those to be deleted, email us.

AI and machine learning

We do not use Google Workspace data, or any data obtained through Google APIs, to develop, improve or train generalised or non-personalized AI or machine learning models.

Your choices

You can stop Label Maker's access at any time by uninstalling it (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Label documents it created stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.

Children

Label Maker is not directed at children under 13.
