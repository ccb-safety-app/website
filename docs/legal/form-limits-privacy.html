Form Limits privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what Form Limits for Google Forms ("Form Limits", "we") does with your information. Form Limits is made by Northpine Apps.

What Form Limits can access

  • The Google Form you open it in, and only that form: its questions and responses, to count places and to open or close the form. It changes the form's choices, description, closed message and open or closed state as you set it up.
  • Your Google account email address, to send you the alerts you turn on, and to check whether you have bought Pro.

Form Limits has no access to your Google Drive, Sheets or Gmail inbox.

What we do with it

Responses are used only to count places and decide whether the form is open. If you turn on waitlist emails, respondents who land on the waitlist (or later get a place) are emailed from your own Gmail account at the address they gave in the form. We do not run a server that receives your responses, and we do not copy, store, sell or share them.

Your settings and a short activity log are stored in your own Google account and the form, and can be removed by switching limits off and uninstalling the add-on.

Services Form Limits uses

  • Google Forms and Gmail sending, through Google Apps Script, in your own account.
  • Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider and reseller. If you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy processes your payment and email address under its own privacy policy. To confirm your plan, Form Limits sends your license key and account email to Lemon Squeezy.

Permissions Form Limits asks for, and why

These are the 6 permissions Google shows when you install Form Limits. It asks for exactly these and nothing else; each is used only for the purpose below.

  • See and edit this form only (forms.currentonly): reads the one form you open Form Limits in (questions, choices, responses) to count places, and changes only that form: choice labels such as "(3 left)", the live line in the description, the closed-form message and whether it accepts responses. It cannot open any other form.
  • Display and run the add-on in Google Forms (script.container.ui): shows the sidebar, the large window and the Extensions menu entry.
  • Create and manage triggers (script.scriptapp): sets up the timers that open and close the form, update the counts when a response arrives and run the hourly check. It removes them when you switch limits off.
  • Send email as you (script.send_mail): sends only the emails you turn on: the waitlist email to a respondent and alerts to you. It cannot read your inbox.
  • Connect to an external service (script.external_request): checks your Pro license with Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. Only the license key and your account email are sent.
  • See your email address (userinfo.email): used for your alerts and to tie a Pro license to your account.

Google user data

Form Limits' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your permission, for security reasons, or where required by law.

How we protect your data

  • Your data stays in your Google account. Form Limits runs on Google Apps Script inside your own Google account. Your form, its responses and its settings are stored and processed only by Google's own systems, which encrypt data in transit and at rest. Northpine Apps has no server that receives, copies or stores them.
  • Encrypted connections only. The only request Form Limits makes outside Google is the license check with Lemon Squeezy, over HTTPS. It sends your license key and account email, never responses, names or form content.
  • Least access. Form Limits asks for the narrowest permissions it can. Its form permission covers only the form you open it in. It can send only the emails you switch on (waitlist and owner alerts), and it has no access to your Drive, Sheets, contacts or inbox.
  • Private settings. Your limits and activity log are kept in the add-on's script storage for your form. Your license key and plan are kept in Google's per-user script storage, which only your own account can read. Respondents cannot see them, and neither can we.
  • No human access. Nobody at Northpine Apps can see your Google data. We would only ever look at data you choose to send us yourself, for example in a support email.
  • Protected developer accounts. The accounts we use to build, sell and publish our add-ons are protected with strong, unique passwords and two-step verification wherever the service offers it.
  • If something goes wrong. If we learn of a security problem that affects your data, we will fix it promptly and tell affected users by email.

Keeping and deleting data

Form Limits does not keep a copy of your Google data. It stores your settings, a counter state for each limited choice, references to waitlisted responses (response IDs, not names or emails) and an activity log of the most recent 30 lines inside your own form's add-on storage. Switch Limits Off removes the timers, counters and waitlist references and puts every choice back. Deleting the form deletes everything the add-on stored with it, and removing the add-on's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops it reading or changing the form. Your license key stays in your account's private storage until you choose Remove Key in the plan panel. A license check result is cached by Google for up to six hours, then expires. Emails the add-on sends are in your own Gmail Sent folder, which you control. Purchase records are kept by Lemon Squeezy as required for tax and accounting; to ask for those to be deleted, email us.

AI and machine learning

We do not use Google Workspace data, or any data obtained through Google APIs, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine learning models.

Your choices

Choose "Switch limits off" in the add-on to put every choice back and remove its timers. You can stop all access by uninstalling it or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Form Limits is not directed at children under 13.
