Certificate Maker privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what Certificate Maker for Google Sheets ("Certificate Maker", "we") does with your information. Certificate Maker is made by Northpine Apps.

What Certificate Maker can access

  • The Google Sheet you open it in (and only that spreadsheet), to read the people and details you want on certificates, and to write each certificate's link, number and status back.
  • A Google Slides design of your own, only if you choose it with the Google file picker, to build certificates from it. Certificate Maker can open only that one file, not your other presentations.
  • Files that Certificate Maker creates in your Google Drive: certificate designs, folders, PDFs and pictures. It does not open or change your other Drive files.
  • Your Google account email address, to show which account you are using, to send you a copy of emails if you ask, and to check whether you have bought Pro.

What we do with it

Your data is used only to make and deliver your certificates. Certificate Maker runs inside your own Google account: we do not run a server that receives your sheet, and we do not copy, store, sell or share the people on it.

When you turn on email, certificates are sent from your own Gmail account to the addresses in your sheet. When you turn on QR codes, each certificate PDF is set so that anyone with its link can view it, so the QR code can open it.

Your settings are stored in your own Google account and the spreadsheet, and can be removed by uninstalling the add-on.

Services Certificate Maker uses

  • Google Drive, Google Slides and Gmail, through Google Apps Script, in your own account. Each certificate is written by the add-on as a PowerPoint file with its pictures inside, and Google Drive turns it into a Google Slides file that the add-on owns; the PDF is exported by Google Drive.
  • The Google file picker (Google Picker), so you can choose your own Slides design. It runs in your browser and gives Certificate Maker access only to the file you pick.
  • Google's Slides thumbnail service, to show a picture of a certificate in the sidebar and to make PNG copies when you ask.
  • Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider and reseller. If you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy processes your payment and email address under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. To confirm your plan, Certificate Maker sends your license key and account email to Lemon Squeezy.

Permissions Certificate Maker asks for, and why

These are the 7 permissions Google shows when you install Certificate Maker. It asks for exactly these and nothing else; each is used only for the purpose below.

  • See and edit this spreadsheet only (spreadsheets.currentonly): reads the people in the spreadsheet you open Certificate Maker in and writes each certificate's link, number and status back into it.
  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (drive.file): creates and manages only the files Certificate Maker makes itself (the logos folder, the certificate folder, the certificates as Google Slides files, PDFs, PNGs and temporary files) and the one Slides design you choose with the Google file picker. It builds each certificate from those files, places logos, signatures and QR codes, and exports the PDF. It cannot see any of your other Drive files or presentations.
  • Display and run the add-on in Google Sheets (script.container.ui): shows the sidebar, the large window and the Extensions menu entry.
  • Create and manage triggers (script.scriptapp): sets up the automatic certificate for new Google Form responses and the follow-up that sends emails held back by Google's daily limit.
  • Send email as you (script.send_mail): emails each certificate to the person, only when you turn emailing on. It cannot read your inbox.
  • Connect to an external service (script.external_request): checks your Pro license with Lemon Squeezy (license key and account email only) and fetches Google's own Slides thumbnails and Drive file contents.
  • See your email address (userinfo.email): used for the "send me a copy" option and to tie a Pro license to your account.

Google user data

Certificate Maker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your permission, for security reasons, or where required by law.

How we protect your data

  • Your data stays in your Google account. Certificate Maker runs on Google Apps Script inside your own Google account. Your sheet, the people on it and the certificates are stored and processed only by Google's own systems, which encrypt data in transit and at rest. Northpine Apps has no server that receives, copies or stores them. QR codes are drawn inside the add-on, not by an outside service.
  • Encrypted connections only. Every request Certificate Maker makes outside your sheet uses HTTPS: Google's own Slides and Drive services, and the license check with Lemon Squeezy. Only your license key and account email are sent to Lemon Squeezy, never names, emails or certificate contents.
  • Least access. Certificate Maker asks for the narrowest permissions it can. Its spreadsheet permission covers only the spreadsheet you use it in. Its Drive permission (drive.file) covers only files Certificate Maker itself creates, so it cannot see or change your other Drive files. A Slides design of your own is opened only if you pick it with the Google file picker, and then only that one file. It has no access to your inbox or contacts, and it sends email only when you turn emailing on.
  • Private settings. Your settings are kept in the add-on's script storage for your spreadsheet. Your license key and plan are kept in Google's per-user script storage, which only your own account can read. Other people who open the sheet cannot see your key, and neither can we.
  • No human access. Nobody at Northpine Apps can see your Google data. We would only ever look at data you choose to send us yourself, for example in a support email.
  • Protected developer accounts. The accounts we use to build, sell and publish our add-ons are protected with strong, unique passwords and two-step verification wherever the service offers it.
  • If something goes wrong. If we learn of a security problem that affects your data, we will fix it promptly and tell affected users by email.

Keeping and deleting data

Certificate Maker does not keep a copy of your Google data. The certificates, designs, logos, signature pictures and PDFs it makes are ordinary files in your own Google Drive and stay there until you delete them; when you remove a logo or signature in the add-on, or after each certificate is made, the matching temporary file is moved to your Drive trash. The link, number and status it writes next to each person stay in your own sheet until you delete them. Your settings and certificate counters are kept in your own Google account. A run that is in progress is remembered by Google for up to six hours (ten minutes for one automatic certificate), and holds no names or emails, only which design and folder to use. A license check result is cached by Google for up to six hours, then expires. If you turn on QR codes, each certificate PDF can be opened by anyone with its link until you delete the file or change its sharing in Drive. Removing the add-on's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops it reading your sheet or making new files. Purchase records are kept by Lemon Squeezy as required for tax and accounting; to ask for those to be deleted, email us.

AI and machine learning

We do not use Google Workspace data, or any data obtained through Google APIs, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine learning models.

Your choices

You can stop Certificate Maker's access at any time by uninstalling it (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Certificates it created stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.

Children

Certificate Maker is not directed at children under 13. Teachers may use it to make certificates for their students; the student details stay in the teacher's own Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
