Three steps, without leaving your sheet

Label Maker opens in a panel beside your spreadsheet, from the Extensions menu.

  1. 1

    Choose your label sheet

    Pick the Avery number printed on your package of labels, or add your own size.

  2. 2

    Arrange the text

    Label Maker finds the name and address columns for you. Click a column to add it to the label, and check every row in the preview.

  3. 3

    Create labels and print

    You get a Google Doc and a PDF. Print a test page on plain paper first, then print on your labels at actual size.

Help before you print

  • Check addressesLooks up each address with Google Maps and suggests fixes before you print. Nothing changes unless you accept a suggestion.
  • QR codes and barcodesPut a QR code or barcode on every label, made from a column in your sheet (a different one for each label) or the same for all: a link to your shop, an order number, an RSVP link or an asset tag. Code 128 and EAN-13 barcodes are supported, and you choose the size and position.
  • Logos, pictures and a return addressAdd your logo or a picture from a link in each row, a return address in the corner, and cut lines for plain paper.
  • Partly used sheetsStart at any label on the sheet, and print several copies of each label.
  • Printer alignment test pagePrint outlines on plain paper, hold them against your label sheet, and shift the layout if your printer is off.

QR codes and barcodes on every label

Label Maker makes them itself, from a column in your sheet (a different code on each label) or the same code on every label. Choose the size, and whether the code sits beside or below the address.

Your addresses stay in your Google account

Label Maker runs inside your own Google account. It reads only the sheet you open it in and the label documents it creates. We don't run a server that receives your sheet, and we don't copy, store, sell or share your addresses.
