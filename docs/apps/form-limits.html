Sign-up forms that never overbook.

Form Limits gives each choice in your Google Form a number of places: sessions, time slots, seats. Full choices close themselves, people see "3 left", and anyone who gets in a second too late goes on a waitlist.

Free on one form, forever

Everything a sign-up form needs

  • Places per choiceSet a limit for each choice, or the same for all. Full choices disappear, or become a "join the waitlist" option.
  • A real waitlistIf two people take the last place at the same moment, the later one is told they're on the waitlist. If a place frees up, they get it automatically and are emailed.
  • Response limit and scheduleClose after a number of responses, open and close at set times, or keep weekly hours with a fresh start each week.
  • "12 places left. Closes Friday."A line under your form's description, kept up to date for you.
  • Emails when it mattersWhen the form closes, when a choice fills, or when it's 80% full.
  • QR code for postersDownload a QR code and short link for your form.

Counts you can rely on

Many limit add-ons keep a running count, which drifts when people submit at the same time or when you delete a response. Form Limits counts your real responses every time, one update at a time, so the numbers are always right. Your "go to section" jumps are kept when choices change.
