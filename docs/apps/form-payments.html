Take payments with the Google Form you already use.

Form Payments sends everyone who fills in your form a clean, itemised payment request with Stripe and PayPal buttons. It keeps track of who has paid and politely reminds the rest. The money goes straight to your own account.

See pricing

How it works

For camps, classes, club dues, tickets, orders and donations.

  1. 1

    Connect Stripe or PayPal

    Use your own accounts. Payers can pick either one, and pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal.

  2. 2

    Set your prices

    Price each choice (Adult $20, Child $10), multiply by a quantity, and add fees, discount codes, tax or a "choose your amount" question. A live example shows the total.

  3. 3

    Watch payments arrive

    Paid status updates by itself. Reminders go to people who haven't paid, and receipts to people who have.

Built for the people who chase payments

  • Automatic remindersEvery few days, up to three times, only to people who haven't paid. No more awkward follow-up emails.
  • Cash and check tooOne click marks a response as paid in person, closes the online links and sends a receipt.
  • Stripe and PayPal togetherOffer both. When one is paid, the other is switched off automatically.
  • Monthly or yearly duesTurn on Monthly or Yearly and members are charged automatically through Stripe.
  • A Payments sheetEvery response, amount, status and payment date in a spreadsheet it creates for you, color-coded.
  • Prices shown on the formPrices appear under the questions, and a "check your email" message shows after submitting.

An email people trust

Itemised, with a clear total and two pay buttons. Payers pay on Stripe's or PayPal's own secure page, and card details never touch the add-on.
